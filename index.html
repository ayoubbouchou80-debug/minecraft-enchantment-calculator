⚒ How the XP Optimizer Works

  1. Select the exact item you want to enchant.
  2. Select every book and its level.
  3. The website checks incompatible enchantments.
  4. Books are merged before the final application whenever possible.
  5. The calculator tracks the prior-work penalty of merged books.
  6. The final step applies the combined book to your item.
  7. If an operation reaches 40 levels or more, it is marked TOO EXPENSIVE.
