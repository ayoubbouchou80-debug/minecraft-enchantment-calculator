✨ Modern XP Methods

TRADING

Villager Trading

Trading with villagers gives experience while also allowing you to obtain useful items. Librarians, farmers, masons and other profession villagers can be incorporated into an XP setup.

VILLAGERS

Glass Pane Trading

A useful trading setup can combine glass production with librarian or cartographer villagers. Sell glass panes through the appropriate villager trades and gain XP from the trades.

FARM

Armadillo / Scute Farming

Armadillos can be used in setups designed around obtaining armadillo scutes. Combine the drops with other resources depending on your server version and farm design.

MOB FARM

Mob XP Farm

Spawner-based farms and general mob farms can provide XP together with useful mob drops.

⚒ How to Reduce Anvil XP Cost

  1. Do not combine every book directly onto the item.
  2. Combine books together strategically first.
  3. Prefer combining books of the same enchantment level when upgrading them.
  4. Keep track of previous-work penalties.
  5. Apply expensive combinations in an efficient order.
  6. Avoid reaching the anvil's Too Expensive limit.
