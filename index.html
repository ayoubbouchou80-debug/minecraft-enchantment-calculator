⚒ How the Optimizer Works

  1. Select your exact item.
  2. Choose the enchantment level for every book you own.
  3. The website checks incompatible enchantments.
  4. The optimizer combines books before applying them where that reduces the work penalty.
  5. It displays the exact sequence: LEFT + RIGHT → RESULT.
  6. Every operation is checked against the 39-level Survival limit.
  7. The plan also tracks prior-work penalties.
