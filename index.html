⚒ How to Reduce Anvil XP Cost

  1. Combine books together strategically.
  2. Avoid repeatedly applying individual books directly to the item.
  3. Combine equal enchantment levels when upgrading books.
  4. Consider the previous-work penalty.
  5. Put expensive combinations in an efficient order.
  6. Avoid reaching the Too Expensive limit.
