📖 Minecraft Enchantment Guide

Minecraft enchantments give your weapons, armor and tools special abilities. However, some enchantments cannot be used together on the same item.

What does this calculator do?

Select your Minecraft item and choose the enchantments you want. The calculator checks whether your selected enchantments can coexist and shows conflicts.

Why are enchantment conflicts important?

Some enchantments are mutually exclusive. For example, Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods cannot normally be combined on the same weapon.
